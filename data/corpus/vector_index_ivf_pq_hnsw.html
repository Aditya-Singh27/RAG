Comparing Vector Index Algorithms

Approximate Nearest Neighbor (ANN) search relies on specialized vector indexing structures.

HNSW (Hierarchical Navigable Small World)

HNSW builds a multi-layer proximity graph where upper layers have long-range skips and lower layers have dense local connections. HNSW provides exceptional recall (95-99%) and low query latency (1-3ms), but consumes immense RAM (typically 1.5x to 2x the raw vector size) and has slow index build times.

IVF-PQ (Inverted File with Product Quantization)

IVF-PQ clusters vectors into Voronoi cells and quantizes vectors into small codebooks. IVF-PQ can operate directly from disk or compressed memory, reducing RAM requirements by 80-95% at the expense of a modest reduction in recall (85-92%) and slightly higher latency (5-15ms).
